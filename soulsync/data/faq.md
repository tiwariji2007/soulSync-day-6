# SoulSync FAQ

## What is SoulSync?

SoulSync is a personal wellness application that helps users record their moods, write journal entries, and reflect on their emotional wellbeing.

## How do I create an account?

Open the Sign up page and register with your email address and a password. After signing up you can log in from the Login page.

## What can I do with mood tracking?

Mood tracking lets you record how you feel, with an optional note, so you can review your mood history and understand your emotional patterns over time.

## What is the journal feature?

The journal feature lets you write and save personal journal entries with a title and written content. You can view your past entries on the History page.

## What are Weekly Insights?

Weekly Insights summarizes your mood entries and journal entries from the last seven days. It shows mood counts, your most frequent mood, mood distribution, recurring journal themes, and a reflection prompt. The written summary is generated by an AI model and appears progressively while it is being written.

## Who can see my moods and journal entries?

Your moods and journal entries are private to your account. Every request is checked against your login session, and Weekly Insights only reads your own entries.

## Is SoulSync a replacement for therapy?

No. SoulSync is a self-reflection tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified mental health professional.

## What does SoulSync Pro include?

SoulSync Pro is a paid upgrade that you can purchase from the Upgrade page. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay in Indian rupees (INR).

## How does the FAQ assistant answer questions?

The FAQ assistant searches this FAQ for the most relevant sections and answers only from them. If the FAQ does not contain the answer, it replies "I don't know."
